Cosmo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Cosmo is a space strategy game made by SUTBeast ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email clashofthecosmos1@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play offline without an account. Offline progress stays on your device.
  • If you play online, we store your email address, your username and your game progress so you can sign in and play against other players.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Information we collect

When you create an online account:

  • Email address and password. Used to sign you in and to recover your account. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read.
  • Username. Shown to other players on leaderboards, in friends lists and in battles.

While you play online:

  • Game data. Your base, resources, trophies, star level, friends list, and the results of battles you fight or defend.
  • Activity times. When you were last online and whether your base is currently under attack, so the game can match battles fairly.
  • Technical information. Like any online service, our servers receive your IP address and basic connection details when the game talks to them. These are kept in short-term server logs for security and fixing problems.

Live battles: live battles connect your device directly to your opponent's device. This means the two devices can see each other's IP address, which can reveal a rough location such as a city or internet provider. Live battles only happen when you choose to join one.

Offline play: if you play offline, your progress is saved only on your device. We don't receive it.

Advertising

The game may offer optional ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with the ad, to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the EU), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or bank details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made, so we can give you what you bought.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and save your progress.
  • To match you with other players, run battles and show leaderboards and friends.
  • To keep the game fair and secure, and to fix bugs.
  • To reply to you if you contact us.

Under UK and EU data protection law, we rely on the need to provide the game you asked for (contract), our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and fair, and your consent for personalised ads.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, which hosts our servers, database and sign in. It processes data on our behalf and may store it outside the UK, with legal safeguards in place.
  • Google AdMob, for optional ads, as described above.
  • Other players can see your username, trophies, star level and base when they play against you.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, saves, friends and battle history are deleted from our database within 30 days. Short-term server logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider.

Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data at any time. See how to delete your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, move it, or object to how we use it. To do any of these, email clashofthecosmos1@gmail.com from the email address on your account. If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Cosmo is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels to our servers over encrypted connections, and each player can only read and change their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes, we'll also let you know in the game.
