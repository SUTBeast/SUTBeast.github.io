Delete your Clash of the Cosmos account

Clash of the Cosmos is made by SUTBeast. You can ask us to delete your online account and all of its data at any time. You don't need to have the game installed.

How to ask

  1. Send an email to clashofthecosmos1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Clash of the Cosmos account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Clash of the Cosmos, so we know the account is yours.
  3. Include your Clash of the Cosmos username.

We'll confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign in details (email address and password).
  • Your username and profile, including trophies and star level.
  • Your saved bases and game progress.
  • Your friends list and your battle history.

Short-term server logs, which may include your IP address, are deleted automatically by our hosting provider. Any purchases can't be restored after the account is deleted.

Offline progress

Progress from offline play is stored only on your device. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings.
